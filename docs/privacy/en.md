# privacy policy — girasol

updated: october 26, 2026. controller: kisnner obando, managua, nicaragua. contact: kisnnerobando7@gmail.com.

girasol has no accounts, ads or advertising tracking. preferences, profile, goals, habit history, scores and weather cache are stored on the device. motion gestures are processed locally. we do not send health data or motion readings to developer servers.

with permission, Health provides water, calories, activity, daylight, heart rate, oxygen, sleep and body data to display records and suggest goals. girasol only writes water, calories and breathing sessions you log. you can deny or revoke access. Health and system backups may sync data according to your apple settings.

weather requests send coordinates rounded to two decimal places to open-meteo over https. open-meteo also receives the connection ip and may retain logs containing coordinates for up to 90 days for troubleshooting and abuse prevention. we do not send your name or Health data. location also orients the shade compass locally.

complications read a weather, water, calories and steps summary through a keychain group shared with the app. updates replace this summary; it may remain after uninstalling. it is keychain protected and is not migrated to another device. game center is disabled in version 1.0.

you can change permissions in apple privacy settings and delete records in Health. uninstalling removes the local app container, but not Health records or necessarily keychain items. local habit history retains up to 60 days. contact us about privacy rights or questions; for open-meteo logs contact info@open-meteo.com.

if you email support, we receive your address and the content you choose to share. it is used to respond and retained as needed to handle your request; you may request deletion. avoid sending unnecessary medical data. external links have their own privacy policies.
