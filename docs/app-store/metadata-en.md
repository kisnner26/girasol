## description

girasol brings everyday habits to your apple watch, without an account or subscription.

check the uv forecast, log water and calories, review the records you share from Health and take a moment to breathe with haptic guidance.

- uv, temperature and general sun-protection guidance.
- estimated ambient exposure with accessible methods and sources.
- water and food logging with the digital crown, editable goals and undo.
- steps, heart rate, blood oxygen and sleep records from Health, when available.
- guided breathing and streaks for water and step goals.
- uv, water and food complications.
- wrist games, touch controls and a shade compass.
- english and spanish, with paper and night appearances.

requires watchos 10 or later. Health features depend on permission and available records; weather requires a connection and location. double tap requires compatible hardware and software. games pause when the display dims or you leave the app.

girasol is not a medical device. uv and exposure are estimates, not measurements of your skin or a safe time allowance in sunlight. sleep summaries do not diagnose disorders. consult a healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

weather and uv: open-meteo, cc by 4.0. weather requests send approximate coordinates to the provider. see the privacy policy for technical-log retention.
